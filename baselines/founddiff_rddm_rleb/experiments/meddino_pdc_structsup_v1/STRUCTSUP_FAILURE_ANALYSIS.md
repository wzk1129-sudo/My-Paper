# Structure supervision outcome and failure analysis

The side task learned the chosen clean-structure target, but did not produce a practically meaningful reconstruction gain. Mean L1 over steps 1–500 versus 5501–6000 fell from 0.007026 to 0.001942 at 128 (72.4% decrease), and 0.005001 to 0.001323 at 64 (73.5% decrease). Final predictions are spatially nonconstant: std128=0.029231, std64=0.024993; target std in the last batch is 0.029407/0.025128. Both heads and every upstream path received nonzero finite gradients in all 6000 steps.

At 6k Fast64 PSNR was 40.848106 dB, +0.001366 vs PDC-Gated-BCA and -0.000436 vs E12-BCA. Full-L333 was 40.855538 dB, +0.001174 vs PDC-Gated-BCA and -0.000516 vs E12-BCA. These are thousandth-dB differences, far below the +0.05 dB Fast-dev engineering heuristic. The PDC paired-slice bootstrap CI is [+0.000589, +0.001742] dB with 60.0% positive slices; this is a one-patient slice-level interval, not patient-level evidence of a meaningful improvement. E12-BCA remains slightly ahead.

The trained beta128/beta64 are 0.096034/0.093251, still near the 0.1 initialization. The BCA alphas are 0.200702/0.212096; both residual paths are nonzero. Thus the structure branch is active, but there is no evidence from this run that its learned target materially improves restoration. The clean-image auxiliary L1 was also added under the explicit-item rule; it changes the training objective relative to the parent. Attribution of the tiny delta to structure supervision alone is therefore unsupported. The auxiliary L1 itself remained near 0.008204→0.007989 across the first/last 500-step windows. No follow-up experiment is started here.
